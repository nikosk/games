# Little Chef's Grand Kitchen

**Game:** `games/little-chef-kitchen/`

## Direction

A warm, visual recipe-building game for a child. The player helps Tilly make one dish by dragging clearly illustrated ingredients through a small kitchen and completing each recipe step in order. The first level is a gentle, readable cookbook scene rather than a conveyor, grid, or kitchen editor.

**Player fantasy:** Be Tilly's little kitchen helper and turn a few friendly ingredients into a dish she cannot wait to eat.

## First level: Tilly's Tomato-Cheese Toast

The first recipe is a short sequence with visible cause and effect:

1. Take bread from the ingredient tray and place it in the toaster.
2. Wait for the bread to become browned toast.
3. Take the whole tomato to the cutting board and make a sliced tomato fan.
4. Put cheese on the browned toast.
5. Add the sliced tomato to make the completed tomato-cheese toast.
6. Place the finished toast on the ceramic plate for Tilly.
7. Tilly reacts with delight; a bitten final dish is the closing state.

The equipment is a compact, child-safe counter arrangement: toaster, cutting board with a child-safe knife, ceramic plate, and ingredient tray. Each action should be understandable from the object art and the next highlighted recipe target. The first level should feel calm and forgiving, with large touch-friendly targets and no precision placement requirement.

## Controls and feedback

- Touch or mouse: drag an ingredient or recipe state to the glowing next station.
- Keyboard support may mirror selection and placement for development, but touch remains the primary interaction.
- Show one next-step hint at a time; reject an incorrect drop gently and leave the ingredient available.
- Animate the important transformations: bread browns, tomato slices, cheese lands, toppings assemble, and Tilly celebrates.
- Use compact overlay feedback instead of a large permanent toolbar or editor chrome.

## Visual brief

Warm illustrated cookbook world: hand-painted gouache and cut-paper texture, rounded storybook silhouettes, tactile paper grain, wood and terracotta counter accents, cream ceramics, tomato red, leafy green, toasted honey-brown, and cheerful golden light. Keep the current kitchen background as the scene anchor and the existing Tilly rabbit as the character anchor. Food and equipment must remain crisp and recognizable at their actual tablet display size.

Art source sheets are intentionally grouped into four carefully spaced transparent Seedream outputs and cropped locally into runtime files:

- equipment: toaster, cutting board and child-safe knife, ceramic plate, ingredient tray in a precise 2x2 layout;
- ingredients: bread, whole tomato, and cheese in a precise 1x3 layout;
- recipe states: browned toast, sliced tomato fan, toast with cheese, completed tomato-cheese toast, and bitten final dish in a precise 1x5 layout;
- Tilly reactions: waiting Tilly, delighted Tilly, heart flourish, and sparkle flourish in a precise 2x2 layout.

Every source-sheet cell has generous even padding and a clear silhouette. No labels or rendered interface text are part of the art; all text belongs to the game UI.

## Smallest fun version

- one compact kitchen scene
- one complete tomato-cheese-toast recipe
- large draggable ingredients and visible step transformations
- waiting and delighted Tilly reactions
- a satisfying plated ending with a bitten final dish

Add more recipes only after this first path is pleasant and easy to understand.

## Sound and feel

Use light, friendly feedback: toaster pop, soft chopping and cheese placement sounds, a warm completion chime, and a cheerful celebratory flourish. Keep the mood relaxed and encouraging rather than timed or stressful.

## Ready for the portfolio when

A child can complete Tilly's Tomato-Cheese Toast comfortably on a tablet, understand every recipe step without reading a manual, see the ingredients transform, and enjoy Tilly's delighted plated reaction.
