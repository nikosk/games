Use case: illustration-story. Create a professional character design reference sheet for Bunny's Picnic, an original painted storybook tablet adventure for a three-year-old. Wide landscape sheet on warm ivory paper, no lettering. Four clearly separated columns: a warm cream bunny with long expressive ears wearing simple raspberry dungarees; a small golden duck with orange beak and feet wearing a soft sage neckerchief; a floppy-eared honey-brown puppy wearing a muted blue short-sleeved shirt; a small round hedgehog with a cream face and rounded chestnut spines wearing a simple ochre vest. Main full-body three-quarter standing pose for each character, consistent size relationships, Bunny tallest, hedgehog smallest. Below each main pose two smaller purpose-made expressive full-body poses: Bunny reaching with one paw then joyful; Duck stretching a wing towards something then laughing; Puppy waiting eagerly then surprised by a leaf on its nose; Hedgehog cautiously peeking then delighted wearing a leaf hat. Warm hand-painted gouache with subtle paper grain and restrained coloured-pencil contours; solid clear silhouettes, simple rounded limbs and readable friendly faces, no tiny fur detail, no fuzzy disappearing edges, shapes practical for separately painted animation parts. Beautiful coherent authored children's book illustration, soft gentle light, restrained natural colours with sufficient character contrast. No franchise characters, no text, no logos, no borders, no scenery, no props besides described leaf. This is visual development artwork, not a sprite atlas.

Use the preceding character reference sheet as the identity and painting style reference: exactly the same cream Bunny in raspberry dungarees and golden Duck in sage neckerchief. Wide landscape composition approximately 5:3, image fills screen, no tablet device frame. An inviting sunlit garden pond in gouache and coloured pencil with paper texture, distant soft trees and a little fence, a very spacious grassy bank across left and lower half, calm blue water on right. Bunny stands on bank at left centre, looking at Duck. Duck stands nearer water at centre and clearly stretches a wing towards a large coral-and-cream striped ball floating just beyond reach on right, isolated against uncluttered blue water. Character faces and poses are legible, large enough to matter; Bunny about one third scene height, Duck proportional to reference. Reeds and a few daisies frame edges, never obscure the ball or characters. A winding path exits left with a small wooden sign displaying only a painted picnic blanket and basket picture, no letters. Integrate a compact floating rounded cream inventory tray at bottom left with three generous slots; first slot contains one recognisable long-handled scoop net icon with a warm selection border, other two empty. Net handle brown, oval sage rim, visible mesh. Tray occupies under a third screen width and under one eighth screen height, scene extends behind it. At top right one small cream circular help button with Bunny portrait and small pictorial speech bubble, no question mark or text. This is a coherent game art mockup, not a book page: clear touchable shapes, obvious spatial story, subdued background detail, beautifully painted warm light, joyful and calm. No text, words, numerals, logos, borders, title, watermark, other characters or extra balls. Do not draw a net in Bunny's hands yet; it is selected in inventory. Preserve reference character clothing, colouring, face shapes and medium. Draw only the single pond screen.
