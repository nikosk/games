Sneak through a moonlit kitchen as a tiny mouse. Watch the cat's patrol and sight line, hide in the mug, kick the spoon as a distraction, steal the special cheese from the counter, and escape through the vent on the left wall. Keyboard controls: A/D or Arrow keys to move, Space or Up to jump, E to hide, steal, or escape, F to kick the spoon, G for fullscreen, Escape to pause. Touch controls: on-screen move, jump, use, and kick buttons.
