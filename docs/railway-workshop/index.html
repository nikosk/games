Railway Workshop: every route is a freshly generated puzzle. Choose straight or curved track, place pieces on the grass, and tap placed track to rotate it. Run the train with the red button; when it arrives, NEXT ROUTE builds a fresh puzzle, and the arrow buttons revisit earlier routes from this session. Keyboard shortcuts: 1 and 2 choose track, E chooses the eraser, Control Z undoes, R clears the board, and Space runs the train.
