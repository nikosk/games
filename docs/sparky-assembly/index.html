Program the toy robot Sparky to carry the gear bin onto the striped dock, then drop it. Command buttons append Forward, Turn Left, Turn Right, or Grab and Drop to the next belt slot. Tap a filled slot to remove it. Use Play, Step, Undo, or Clear to test and edit the program. Keyboard shortcuts: W or Up adds Forward; A or Left adds Turn Left; D or Right adds Turn Right; G adds Grab or Drop; Space or Enter plays; S or Period steps; Backspace or Delete undoes; C clears; and F toggles fullscreen. Sound and fullscreen controls are also available on screen.
