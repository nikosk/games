The original assets use bold preschool-cartoon shapes. Production rooms now use the selected handcrafted paper-theatre direction: layered paper, felt toys, painted wood, embroidered details, and shallow material shadows. They do not contain copied television characters or locations. No attribution requirement was supplied by the generation API.

Twelve successful image calls have been made for a total OpenRouter cost of **$0.48**. The rich-room expansion added one Seedream 4.5 call costing **$0.04**, followed by three art-direction studies costing **$0.12**. Four production-study calls added the Counting Menagerie room, paper puzzle stage, fairy, and a corrected room variant for **$0.16**. The first counting-room result was rejected because it contained misleading numerals; the corrected result had three remaining train digits removed locally. Two early watercolor drafts were also rejected and are not kept. Failed wrapper invocations were not charged.
