# Style study A — heirloom gouache storybook

Asset type: Full-screen game background style study
Purpose: Compare a richer, higher-quality visual direction for a 1280x720 tablet puzzle room with eight large clickable stations
Subject: A magical toyshop at night, viewed straight-on. A tall empty moon door stands exactly in the center. Eight large separate toy displays surround it: teddy shelf and moon clock above left, block chest and ballerina music box below left, star mobile and dollhouse above right, toy train and jack-in-the-box below right. Add warm plank flooring, a woven oval rug, carved shelves, garlands, tiny golden sparks, and a crescent window.
Style: Heirloom children's picture-book illustration painted in opaque gouache with confident ink contours, softly imperfect brush edges, layered matte pigment, subtle tooth of cold-press paper, carefully observed painted wood and fabric, warm handmade character, sophisticated but inviting for ages 3–5. Richer and more authored than flat vector clip art. Clear silhouettes and restrained detail around interactive toys.
Composition: Straight-on 16:9 stage, symmetrical enough to read instantly but charmingly handmade. Keep the empty central arch clear from x 42–58% and y 10–82%. Keep all eight objects distinct, non-overlapping, and mostly within x 10–90%. Each object should read at 120–180 pixels. Decorative detail stays behind the stations.
Palette and light: Plum, mulberry and midnight-blue walls; honey wood; parchment cream; faded coral, celadon, butter yellow and dusty sky blue. Cool moonlight from the left, warm candle-gold pools around toys, gentle value depth without muddy darkness.
Reference images: The attached current rich room defines only the required object list, central-door placement, and straight-on layout. Do not imitate its flat digital-vector rendering.
Must preserve: Empty central arch, exactly eight obvious large toy stations, tablet-safe composition, warm non-threatening magic
Constraints: No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, people, copied characters, recognizable franchises, photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic look, flat vector clip art, tiny clutter, puzzle answers embedded in the room
Background: Opaque complete room scene
