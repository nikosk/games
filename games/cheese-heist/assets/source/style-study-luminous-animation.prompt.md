# Style study C — luminous animated fairytale

Asset type: Full-screen game background style study
Purpose: Compare a cinematic, high-quality visual direction for a 1280x720 tablet puzzle room with eight large clickable stations
Subject: An enchanted toyshop just after midnight, viewed straight-on. A tall empty moon door stands exactly in the center. Eight large separate toy displays surround it: teddy shelf and moon clock above left, block chest and ballerina music box below left, star mobile and dollhouse above right, toy train and jack-in-the-box below right. Add warm floorboards, a plush oval rug, shelves fading into painted atmosphere, star garlands, floating dust and a crescent window.
Style: Premium hand-painted 2D feature-animation background, with expressive shape design, softly sculpted painted forms, elegant colored-pencil accents, refined atmospheric depth, luminous practical light and crisp readable focal objects. Whimsical European fairytale mood without imitating any studio or existing film. Detailed and cinematic but never busy, dark, realistic or generic vector art.
Composition: Straight-on 16:9 theatrical room with subtle depth. Keep the empty central arch clear from x 42–58% and y 10–82%. Use light and value grouping to isolate eight large non-overlapping stations. Keep them mostly within x 10–90% and readable at 120–180 pixels.
Palette and light: Deep indigo, blackberry and wine walls; amber wood; moonlit cream; jewel coral, turquoise, leaf green and saffron toys. Silver-blue moonlight pours from upper left while each toy catches a soft golden magical rim light. Rich shadows retain color and child-friendly warmth.
Reference images: The attached current rich room defines only the required object list, central-door placement, and straight-on layout. Do not imitate its flat vector finish.
Must preserve: Empty central arch, exactly eight obvious large toy stations, luminous story focus, safe cozy mood, tablet readability
Constraints: No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, people, copied characters, recognizable franchises, photorealism, 3D rendering, flat vector clip art, horror, murky black shadows, tiny clutter, puzzle answers embedded in the room
Background: Opaque complete room scene
