# Little Chef’s Cake Workshop

Make a cake that is yours all the way through. Mix two sponges with different flavours and little extras, spread a filling, pipe icing and place decorations, then cut a slice to reveal the layers.

Tap ingredients and tools; stir, spread, and pipe with a finger or mouse. No timers, scores, orders, or wrong combinations. Mixing is short; decorating is open-ended. Warm cream, raspberry, mint, and hand-drawn patisserie shapes. Large cake, small controls, immediate feedback.

First playable version: round two-layer cakes, three sponge flavours, two optional mix-ins, three fillings, coloured frosting and piping, sprinkles, berries, flowers, candles, undo, slicing, and a local cake shelf. Cakes on the shelf stay in this browser.
